        Common questions

        Is this really free?

        Yes. No cost, no catch, and no obligation to do anything with it.

        Do I have to sell or list my park?

        No. Plenty of owners use the plan to raise income and keep running the park. Others use the number for refinancing, estate planning or a partner buyout.

        What if I don't know all my numbers?

        Round numbers are fine. If you leave expenses blank, I estimate them from typical campground operating costs and tell you I did.

        How accurate is the estimate?

        It's a solid starting range, not an appraisal. Your real value depends on things a form can't see, like your books, your market and the condition of the park. Your free report narrows it down.

        Why do you ask how I run the park?

        That's where most of the hidden value is. Things like metered electric, peak-weekend pricing and seasonal rates can add hundreds of thousands to what a park is worth.

        What happens to my park if I sell to you?

        I know you built something. Before anything is signed, we'll sit down and talk about what matters to you: the name, your staff, your seasonal families, and how the park runs. Plenty of parks are better off with new energy and the same heart.

        Will you sell my information?

        No. Your numbers stay with me. I only bring in a partner buyer if you ask me to.

        What happens after I send it?

        Your full report opens on the page right away. If you'd like, I'll set up a short call to walk through it. No pressure either way.
